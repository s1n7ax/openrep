# OpenRep privacy policy

_Effective 23 October 2026._

OpenRep is a free, open-source gym companion app for Android and iOS, made by
the OpenRep project (https://github.com/s1n7ax/openrep). This policy covers
every build of the app: Google Play, F-Droid, the APKs on GitHub Releases and
builds from source.

## The short version

OpenRep collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and
no tracking, and it does not share anything with anyone.

## Data on your device

Your plans, custom exercises, workout history and settings are stored only on
your device, in the app's private storage. Phone backups (Google, iCloud) do
not include them. They leave the device only when you export a backup
(Settings → Backup), and the backup file goes wherever you choose to save or
share it.

Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

## Network connections

The app goes online for two things only:

- **Catalog updates.** When the device is online, the app downloads the
  latest exercise catalog and exercise photos from GitHub
  (`raw.githubusercontent.com`). These requests send no information about you
  or your data. As with any web request, GitHub receives your IP address and
  handles it under the
  [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **When you open GitHub yourself.** Submit (to share a plan or a custom
  exercise with the project) and the repository link open GitHub in your
  browser. What you then do on GitHub is covered by GitHub's privacy
  statement, not by the app.

A Content-Security-Policy in the app blocks every other network connection.

## Permissions

On Android the app asks only for network access (`INTERNET`), for the catalog
updates above.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
